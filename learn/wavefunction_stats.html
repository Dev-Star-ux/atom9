Wavefunction Statistics

Born rule in a 1-D box: 2D shows ψ / |ψ|² charts; 3D is a measurement chamber where detections fall onto a detector. AI discovers box width a.

1. Principle

Idea. Born rule: each measurement finds the particle at one random x. After many detections the histogram converges to |ψ_n(x)|². 2D is the Wavefiz-style plot; 3D is a chamber — glowing lobes mark likely regions, amber hits fall and stack on the detector.

2. Key formulas

P(x) dx = |ψ(x)|² dx
  • Identify each symbol in the formula and its unit.
  • Ask which quantity you control in the simulator, and which you measure.

3. What to observe

In the sim. 2D: toggle ψ ↔ |ψ|² and watch amber bars fill the cyan curve. 3D: fire detections — spheres rain into the bright lobes and build the same pattern.

4. Step-by-step reasoning

Step A. Start from the physical picture (particles, waves, fields, or levels) described above.
Step B. Write the governing relation: P(x) dx = |ψ(x)|² dx
Step C. Change one control at a time in the experiment and predict the sign of the change before you look.
Step D. When an AI panel is present, collect data and compare the learned constant with the accepted value.

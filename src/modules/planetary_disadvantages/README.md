# Planetary Model — Disadvantages (Ch.2 §4 T1)

Three classical failures of the Rutherford planetary atom.

## Principle
Classical electrodynamics predicts radiative collapse, continuous spectra, and
no preferred orbit radius — all contradicted by experiment.

## Problems
1. **Radiation** — electron spirals in, energy bar drains
2. **Spectrum** — classical rainbow vs sharp H lines
3. **Stability** — many arbitrary orbit sizes, no selection rule

## The AI — infers collapse rate
From noisy energy-decay samples during radiative collapse, estimates the
classical energy-loss rate (%/s).

## Files
- `physics.js` — problem animations, H lines, wavelength colour
- `fit.js` — GridSearch1D on decay rate
- `module.js` — problem chips + animated canvas

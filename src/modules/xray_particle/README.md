# X-Ray Particle Nature (Ch.2 §5)

keV X-rays as photons: E = hc/λ. Photoelectric ejection when E > binding W.

## Principle
One photon → at most one photoelectron. Energy balance: E = W + KE.

## The AI — discovers hc
Grid search on hc from noisy (E, λ) photon pairs.
